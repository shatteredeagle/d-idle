## Why

The user said a left click skips NPC speech; NPCSKIP built four skip strategies but nothing proved which works on a first contact in the original game.

## What Changes

Live probe on three fresh characters (Freshb, Freshc, Freshd): 12 first-contact visits across the four strategies. No strategy skipped the speech (the pointer never hovers during it); the greeting starts during the approach, 12 to 35 s for Akara and Gheed, mostly over for Charsi and Kashya. Default town.greetingSkip set to 0 (wait): it outlasts the speech and sends no useless clicks. The user's claim was not reproduced with our input path.
