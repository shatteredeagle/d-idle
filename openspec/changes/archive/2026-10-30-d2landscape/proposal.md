## Why

The user asked whether any other decompilation or reimplementation project is a better fit than d2d for the bot's test and product backend.

## What Changes

Docs only: docs/research/d2-landscape.md surveys every serious Diablo II reimplementation, decompilation, emulation and bot project and scores them on the six backend needs. Verdict: none beats d2d; jaenster/d2-dedicated-server is the strongest complement (an original-logic headless server, real time only), libd2 has a verified map generator, Kolbot/D2BS are prior art.
