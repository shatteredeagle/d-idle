## Why

Briefs are rewritten by hand each time, ownership and shared resources (socket, bot binary, lab binary, fork branches) live only in prose, and STATUS and INDEX rot within hours. A thin change card layer in OpenSpec-compatible form makes the brief a card id and lets a script check overlap.

## What Changes

- openspec/ layout, README, seven seeded specs
- change cards for the running and just-finished jobs and archive entries for tonight merges
- cmd/changecheck and the generated STATUS block
- docs/OWNERS, docs/adr/, doc fixes and stale banners, AGENTS.md update, spec-sme agent, trial notes
