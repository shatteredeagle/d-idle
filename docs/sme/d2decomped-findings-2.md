## 1. Combat formulas (`re/combat.md`, `re/char-panel.md`, `re/party.md`)

**To-hit** (`FUN_0057ec10` -> `FUN_0057d9b0`; D2D-V)
- Defense used: base defense + stat 33 (vs melee) or stat 32 (vs missiles).
- Player attack rating (`FUN_00622560`) = stat 19 + (dex - 7) x 5 + class ToHitFactor, then x (100 + weapon-mastery to-hit + stat 119 + the skill's ToHit bonus)/100 (+ stat 0xb3 vs monster types). Monster AR = stat 19 + skill bonus, x (100 + stat 119)/100 (their `dex x 5` term does not apply: MonStats monsters have no dexterity).
- `c = AR x 100 / (AR + DEF)`; then `c = c x 2 x alvl / (alvl + dlvl)`; clamp **5..95**; hit when attacker seed % 100 < c. Percent first, then the level factor (integer truncation), negative defense adds to AR.
- Consequence for the bot: against a player the minimum hit chance is 5 % and the maximum 95 % no matter the defense, and a high-level monster vs low-level player gets the 2x alvl/(alvl+dlvl) factor, which can push c above its AR/(AR+DEF) base (alvl > dlvl).
- A melee swing at a player who is in mode 3 (RN, running) skips the roll and lands (`FUN_0057ec10`); missiles still roll. D2D-V (the note states it; I take it as "players that are running cannot be dodged by defense against melee"). Check live (D2D-U for what an animation mode 3 maps to on screen).

**Block** (`FUN_00622720`; D2D-V)
- Players need a shield. Chance = (stat 20 + class BlockFactor) x (dex - 15) / (clvl x 2), clvl at least 1, **max 75**. (Stat 20 is the shield's total block %, see charstats BlockFactor.) Monsters use stat 20 where MonStats allows blocking.
- Chance is cut to one third while the player is walking or running (modes 2/3/6 but "walking keeps it whole" is the note's literal wording for mode 2; running/other modes get a third). My reading of that sentence is D2D-U; verify with a probe if blocking matters.
- After block come, in this order, Weapon Block, **dodge** (stat 338, melee, standing), **avoid** (339, missiles), **evade** (340, moving). All 0 until passives give them.

**Damage** (`FUN_0057b420`, `FUN_0057b7d0`; D2D-V)
- Physical min/max = stats 21/22 (23/24 second weapon; 1/2 barehanded) + stat 111, all 256ths. The weapon's own enhanced damage (stats 17/18) is already folded in at item level.
- One percentage `p` = skill enhanced damage + stat 25 (`damagepercent`) + str x StrBonus/100 + dex x DexBonus/100 + mastery, min -90. min' = min + min x (stat 18 + p)/100, max' = max + max x (stat 17 + p)/100, uniform roll between, x SrcDam/128 (SrcDam 128 = full weapon).
- Deadly strike (stat 141), critical strike (337) and mastery crit are three **separate** rolls against rand(100); any one that hits doubles physical damage (no x3, no stacking).
- Elemental item damage: fire 48/49, lightning 50/51, magic 52/53, cold 54/55 with their masteries (329/330/331/357); poison 57/58 over 59 (poison mastery 332). Conversion records can turn physical into an element (fire/light/magic/cold/poison; cold >= 50 length, poison one eighth a tick).
- Life leech stat 60, mana 62, stamina 64.

**Resists** (`FUN_0057c1e0`, `FUN_0057be00`; D2D-V)
- Damage x (100 - res)/100, res at most 100. Attacker pierce stats (e.g. 335 cold, 336 poison length) subtract from the defender's resist first.
- **Player resist** = stat - pierce, plus the difficulty penalty on all but physical and magic, capped at `75 + max-resist stat` (never above 95; 75 when no max stat), floor **-100**. Penalty in an expansion (LoD) game = DifficultyLevels `ResistPenalty` = **0 / -40 / -100** (Normal / Nightmare / Hell); a classic non-expansion game is hard-coded 0 / -20 / -50 (`re/char-panel.md` ~49; `code:` fight.cpp). For our bot (LoD character) use 0/-40/-100.
- **Monsters have no cap** and take whatever MonStats says (so negative/very high values are possible); the note does not tabulate them (read from MonStats/by difficulty at run time).
- Cold/freeze length use cold resist; poison length uses stat 110 (poison length reduction), poison resist only affects per-tick damage. Cannot-be-frozen (stat 153) zeroes cold/freeze length, half-freeze (118) halves it.
- Physical resist stat 0x24 (damageresist): crushing blow subtracts raw damageresist (100 at most) from its damage, not the 50 % cap of a normal hit.

**Crushing blow, open wounds** (`re/monsters.md` MonProp; D2D-V for CB, D2D-U for OW)
- Crushing blow (stat 136): seed % 100 < value, then the target loses current life / divisor (after the hit), cut by raw physical resist. Divisor **10** against a player or hireling; **4** against another monster, **8** against a boss or unique, plus a player-count share; doubled for missiles. Players' own CB is therefore a 25 % (normal) / 12.5 % (boss) of *current* life - community-consistent.
- The note in `re/combat.md` "Approximations" ("a quarter of current life, no boss divisors") is the older port approximation and is superseded by monsters.md.
- Open wounds: d2d uses 1.10's per-level table for 8 s (`re/combat.md` ponytail): **not traced** for 1.14d. D2D-U.
- Monster knockback/CB/thorns/cast-rate props come from MonProp rows (Hell Blood Raven 60 % knockback; quill rats Hell thorns 15-20 / 30-40; Blunderbore 15-25 CB; Mephisto cast/swing rate). See section 3.

**Hit recovery/animation** (`re/combat.md`, `re/skills.md` FHR section; D2D-U)
- Monster hit recovery in d2d = at an eighth of max life, player a twelfth: approximation. FHR/FBR are approximations too: d2d's formula is **off by 1-4 frames** against community FHR breakpoints at high FHR (the real mechanism is not found in game.exe: `re/skills.md` "Hit-recovery (FHR)"). Do not trust frame counts from d2d for FHR.

**Regeneration** (`re/combat.md`; D2D-V)
- Players: each frame `life += hpregen (stat 74)`, capped at max, and **never below 1 life** (`if life < 0x100: life = 0x100`): poison and burning cannot kill a player. Poison (state 2) and burning (state 0x73) are negative hpregen stats; one of each at a time; a new one replaces the old only if at least as strong. Healing potions are a positive hpregen in their own state, so potion and poison add per tick.
- Monsters: `life += hpregen` clamped at 0; at 0 they die and the kill is credited to the owner of the poison state.
- **Mana per frame (256ths)** = max(maxmana(256ths) / (CharStats ManaRegen x 25), 1) x (100 + stat 27)/100 (+ stat 26 flat), capped at the missing amount; ManaRegen = 120 for every class, so base regen = maxmana/3000 per frame in 256ths = full mana in **120 s at 25 Hz**. Integer per frame, so small pools regain less than the ideal. My worked numbers (formula from the notes, arithmetic mine, D2D-U for rounding at the edges): maxmana 20 -> 1/256 per frame = 0.098/s (ideal 0.167/s); 30 -> 2/256 = 0.195/s (ideal 0.25); 50 -> 4/256 = 0.39/s (ideal 0.417); 100 -> 8/256 = 0.78/s. Mana regen is a flat fraction per second (not per frame count of the frame rate): game runs 25 frames/s.
- No life regen from vitality exists in the notes' regen code except stat 74; natural life regen is not described (D2D-U).

**Potions** (`FUN_005be3f0`, `FUN_005beac0`; D2D-V)
- Healing/mana potion: amount = item calc, `hpregen` << 8 then class multiplier (Amazon/Paladin/Assassin x1.5, Barbarian x2, others x1; mana: Amazon/Paladin/Assassin x1.5, Sorceress/Necromancer/Druid x2); chance (`rand(100) < rand(vit or energy) >> 1`) to double. Applied as a regen state over `len` frames; drinking again while active **joins**: `(old x left + amount)/(left + len)` per frame, lasting `left + len`. So chain-drinking raises the per-frame rate and extends the duration; it is not instantaneous. First harvest section 5 has this.
- Rejuvenation: percentage of max by MulDiv, 100 = full, no class bonus, instant (`FUN_005beac0`).
- Antidote/thawing: +50 poison/cold resist (and +10 max) for 750 frames (30 s), clears poison / freeze. NPC heal and wells clear poison and freeze, not chill.
- Shift+belt gives a potion to the merc (hpot/apot/wpot only).

**Death** is in harvest 1 section 1 (nothing new except: mana/life are then full at respawn in town).

**XP/party** (`re/party.md`; D2D-V): with n players, monster life and **experience** get the same bonus table 0, 0, 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 350 % (index n; n>8: (n-2) x 50 for life, (5n+130) x 2 for xp). NoDrop uses n = near + (players - near)/2. Party exp: a share is awarded only to members alive, same level, within 0x1900 squared subtiles (80 subtiles). Solo bot: no bonus. The merc gets its own share = 86/256 of the penalised award when it is not the killer (`FUN_0057e860`), not simulated in d2d. **The level-difference XP penalty is applied in `FUN_0057e480` but not documented in the notes**: leave our pace tables to our own sources.

## 2. Skills our early game uses (`re/skills.md`; formula machinery D2D-V, per-skill numbers not tabulated)

**Skill record machinery** (D2D-V, traced + emulator diffs)
- Per-level value brackets (`FUN_00644b70`): `*Lev1` applies to levels 2-8, `Lev2` to 9-16, `Lev3` 17-22, `Lev4` 23-28, `Lev5` 29 up (cumulative per-level adders). So skill damage per level is piecewise linear in the five brackets from Skills.txt, not one slope.
- Elemental min/max (`FUN_00644d50/e40`): `(EMin + brackets) << HitShift`, plus the **synergy as a percent of that** (EDmgSymPerCalc, e.g. Fire Bolt: (Fire Ball + Meteor levels) x par8), plus the elemental mastery (stats 0x14a..0x14c: fire 329, lightning 330, cold 331, poison 332) *after* the synergy.
- Duration: `ELen + ELevLen1..3` over levels 2-8, 9-16, 17+ plus a percent.
- To-hit: `ToHit + LevToHit x (lvl-1)` or ToHitCalc (spell missiles with no ToHit simply hit).
- **Mana cost** (`FUN_00647540`): `(mana + lvlmana x (lvl-1)) << manashift` in 256ths, at least `minmana x 256`; a skill short on mana is dropped by the server silently; AttackNoMana skills fall back to a plain attack. Charged item skills cost no mana.
- Calc `dm` ("diminishing"): `a + (b - a) x floor(110 x lvl / (lvl + 6)) / 100`, capped at `b`; `ln`: `a + b x (lvl-1)`. `ulvl` = character level, `blvl` = base (hard) level. Useful when we compute damage from Skills.txt columns ourselves.
- Missile damage is built when the missile is made; weapon damage joins only when SrcDam is non-zero (x SrcDam/128). Missile velocity = `(Vel + VelLev x lvl/8) x 75/100` (`FUN_00648690`), range = Range + RangeLev x lvl.
- **Cast rate (FCR)**: `EFCR = FCR x 120 / (120 + FCR)`, then frames = `ceil(base x 256 / (256 + floor(256 x EFCR/100)))`. Matches the Sorceress community table exactly (checked in the notes). Base SC frames (animdata): Amazon 20, Assassin 17, Barbarian 14, Druid 15, Necromancer 16, Paladin 16, **Sorceress 14**. Sorceress lightning family (Charged Bolt, Nova, Lightning, Chain Lightning, Thunder Storm) uses the S1 anim, also 14 frames; Fire Bolt/Ice Bolt/Frost Nova SC. Breakpoints (FCR -> frames), Sorc and Barb 0->14 9->13 20->12 37->11 63->10 105->9 200->8; Necro/Paladin 0->16 9->15 18->14 30->13 48->12 75->11 125->10 232->9; Amazon 0->20 7->19 14->18 22->17 32->16 48->15 68->14 99->13 152->12; Assassin 0->17 8->16 16->15 27->14 42->13 65->12 102->11 174->10; Druid 0->15 9->14 19->13 32->12 54->11 86->10 152->9. Caveat: note itself says the *call site* is not found and no `imul 0x78` appears in game.exe, so FCR is "emergent/verified by match" rather than traced (D2D-U for mechanism, V for the numeric table matching known community breakpoints).

**Sorceress**
- Fire Bolt, Ice Bolt, Magic Arrow, Ice Blast, Bone Spear, Fire Ball, Lightning built as missile skills (`FUN_0056f7f0` -> `FUN_0059fa30`). Cast on a monster or on ground; pierce stat 328 % lets Lightning/Bone Spear fly through; Cold Mastery-style pierce stats cut the monster resist unless immune, to -100 (the "published rule", not traced).

Does not path around obstacles beyond the standard path types. Pets cross level edges with the owner, traps do not.

## 3. Act 1 Normal monsters: AI and behaviour (`re/monster-ai.md`, `re/monsters.md`, `re/superuniques.md`, `re/act1-end.md`, `re/quests-act1.md`)

General mechanics beyond harvest 1 section 7 (D2D-V unless stated):
- **Driver order** (`FUN_005b1740`): stun/freeze handling (state 0x15 stands 3), door opening (if the MonStats row has opendoors), flee target; then target search by MonAI row (type 1 search, none -> wander/stand; type 2 search only; 4/5 other); first-sight speech; teleport mod; leash (if target beyond the level's byte +0x2f it re-searches or wanders 4); finally the AI's own think.
- **Think rate**: aidel frames (MonStats +0x4f + difficulty, default 15 frames = 0.6 s; Andariel 15/11/9 by difficulty); at once at the end of walk/run modes. A monster can take a target only within 0x37 = 55 subtiles (11 tiles); in practice aidist 35 subtiles.
- **Pathing** (`re/monster-ai.md` "A move"): a straight Bresenham line to the target; if blocked it stops at the last free subtile and takes at most `steps` (5 for most monsters) greedy 8-way steps; re-path only when the target has moved over 5 subtiles from the last path end; **re-path budget 0x14 (20)**; a monster that cannot path stands (`aidel` later). Monsters do not navigate mazes: they are dumb chasers that get stuck behind walls and cliffs. Useful: a caster on the other side of any obstacle drops aggro-chase soon; **fleeing from ranged attackers behind terrain works**.
- **No call for help** (but see Fallen). Being hit does not wake a monster; range and area alerts do (harvest 1).
- **Hit recovery stand/run flags**: a mode end WL(2) and RN(15) think at once, others via NU.
- Monsters never target players in a town room (levels 1, 40, 75, 103, 109); outdoors no LOS needed.
- Monster life = MonLvl[row at area/own level] x MonStats min..max % of the MonLvl HP column (1.14d), + players bonus; level for Nightmare/Hell (LoD) = **area level** unless noRatio or boss; Normal = the monster's own level.

## 6. Waypoints and the area graph (`re/waypoint.md`, `re/act1-end.md` 713-840, `re/drlg.md`)

- **Bitfield**: per player per difficulty 16 bytes: u16 header 0x0102 + 7 u16 words, bit table 0x746428 (112 entries) maps waypoint index i (Levels `Waypoint` column 0..38) to word 1 + i/16, mask 1 << (i%16). So a bot must **click a new waypoint twice** to travel from it; the first click only activates (D2D-V; compare our `docs/d2-1.14d-memory.md` row for TakeWaypoint which assumed a single click: confirm live).

The bot must wait >= 10 s after arriving in an area before clicking a waypoint row, otherwise the click is eaten. Also refused for the current level, an inactive waypoint, an object not in the same act (other act = act change path), or level 0.

## 9. Performance, determinism, save format, memory vs `docs/d2-1.14d-memory.md`

**Determinism and headless** (D2D-V for the game.exe facts, d2d for the tooling)
- game.exe starts a game with `-seed`: the game seed becomes `{map seed, 666}` (`FUN_0052c280`); otherwise it is seeded from QueryPerformanceCounter and the map seed is drawn from that. `FUN_00530930` steps the seed 4 times after seeding: monster regions (`FUN_00547d20`), object seed (`FUN_00546c60`), sunitproxy seed (vendor stock/hire lists, `FUN_00536070`), quests (`FUN_00545d80`). After that **only room population** rolls the game seed inline (`FUN_0054ec90`), and unit seeds are steps of the game seed at unit creation (`{low, 666}`). A drop's seed is the monster's unit seed as its life left it: "nothing on the death path draws on the monster's seed", so a drop is determined by the game seed and the order of kills/room entries. That is how d2d's 100000/100000 drop check works; it means real game.exe drops are not random per kill beyond that history (the bot cannot "reroll" a kill by retrying). With `-seed` and identical inputs/order the whole game is reproducible.
- d2d is a 25 Hz server tick (`re/network.md`: game frames at 1000/fps; flush 40 ms) with `--headless --devctl <socket>` (SDL dummy driver + software renderer) and `--seed N`, `--no-save`, `--no-video`, `--start-*`; verbs include `monsters`, `ground`, `npcs`, `menu`, `items`, `state`, `debug attack` (all derived combat stats), `debug difficulty`, `debug kill/boss/quest/goto/warp` (see `docs/control_channel.md`). A headless smoke runs "all three acts' quests ~1:45"; `tests/play_d2d.py` (a play bot by clicks) is only planned (PLAN.md "Checkpoint 2026-10-14"). Nothing in the notes describes running faster than real time or a fixed-step mode.
- `-seed` is a real game.exe switch (`FUN_0052c280` reads it; `re/deviations.md` Additions says d2d keeps game.exe's own switches). Whether our Wine launch could pass it for reproducible maps is untested (D2D-U); we may not launch games ourselves (AGENTS.md).
- **Save format hints for savecheck** (from d2d's `d2s` reader/writer and `re/unverified.md`; format taken from the community spec, validated on 19 real 1.14d saves, so D2D-U-by-source but strong): header +0x00 magic 0xAA55AA55, +0x04 version (0x5c..0x60 accepted by the host loader `FUN_0056a090`; which of them 1.14d writes is not said in the notes, D2D-U), +0x08 file size, +0x0c checksum (function `FUN_00411130`; host rejects a bad one), +0x14 name[16], +0x24 status (0x04 hardcore, 0x08 died, 0x20 expansion), +0x25 progression, +0x28 class (0..6, header check <= 7), +0x2b level, +0x30 last played, +0x38 hotkeys[16], +0x78/+0x7c left/right skill, +0x88 appearance[16], +0xa8 difficulty[3] (0x80 = active), "Woo!" quest block at +0x14f (u32 version 6, u16 size 298, then 96 bytes per difficulty at +0x159), `WS` waypoints at +0x279 (+6, then 24 bytes per difficulty at +0x283), NPC-intro block at +0x2c9 (tag byte 'w' at +0x2ca, 8 bytes/difficulty intros at +0x2cd, congratulations at +0x2e5), stats/skills/items bit-packed after +0x2fd. After the stats: item list "JM"+count, then the corpse list "JM"+count (harvest 1 section 1), then merc/golem items (that last part is community spec, D2D-U). The host's join-time header check also requires the name at +0x14 equal the game's account name (stricmp), size field = file size. `deviations.md` #6: d2d writes saves only if they parse and checksum is good, keeps a `.bak`. For savecheck: verify magic, version, size == file length, recompute checksum, name, class <= 6 (LoD), progression, quest block signature `Woo!` and `WS`.

## 10. Five findings that change what the bot does (most consequential first)

1. **Waypoint travel is refused for 10 s after any level change** (C->S 0x49, `FUN_0054c5d0`), an unlit waypoint needs a second click, and the panel will not open with an item on the cursor. Add a 10 s post-arrival guard, a "click twice if new" rule and a cursor-empty precondition (`re/act1-end.md` 713-840; D2D-V).
2. **Players cannot die from poison/burning** (life floors at 1 each frame) and mana regen is maxmana/3000 per frame in 256ths with integer truncation (120 s to full, worse for small pools; e.g. 20 mana regains only 0.098/s). Andariel's/Quill rats' poison needs managing as a healing-potion tax, not a death threat; early-game mana regen is much slower than the ideal for tiny pools (`re/combat.md`; D2D-V).
3. **Monsters do not wake when hit, and do not navigate**: outdoors anything within ~35 subtiles takes you, in caves only line-of-sight or one aware monster in the area; their paths are straight lines plus 5 greedy steps, re-path budget 20 (`re/monster-ai.md`; D2D-V, emulator-matched). Fallen packs charge as a group once the leader/shaman rallies; the Countess loses her special AI if her room is freed and reloaded.
4. **Vendor and drop economics are deterministic from the game seed**: store level = clvl+5 capped at 12 (Normal Act 1), stock re-rolled every 4 min (not Kashya/Greiz/Larzuk), buy/sell multipliers and stat 87 reductions, repair = proportional to missing durability, identify 100 gold per item all at once, gamble formula known, gold coins = ilvl + rand(5 ilvl) x TC mul x gold find (`re/store.md`, `re/drops.md`; D2D-V).
5. **Resist penalties for a LoD character are 0 / -40 / -100 and capped at 75 + max (<= 95); monsters are uncapped**; a player's crushing blow divisor is 10, monster CB is 4 (boss 8); damage = base x (1 + p) with deadly strike, critical strike and mastery crit as separate doublers; to-hit clamps to 5..95 % with the level factor 2 x alvl/(alvl+dlvl) (`re/combat.md`; D2D-V).
