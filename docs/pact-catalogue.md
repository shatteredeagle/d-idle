# Pact catalogue (TASK-166)

> status: data, loader and rules built and tested offline; no bot reads it yet (TASK-167 is the first consumer). Design: `docs/research/pact-system.md` (a design note); every heat and reward number is U (tuned in play). Tags: V = proven by a test in this tree, U = unverified.

- Data: `data/pacts/pacts.json` (embedded by `data/pacts/pacts.go`, package `pactdata`; `//go:embed` cannot reach up from `internal/`). Schema = section 4 of the design note: id, name, `lever_family` (bot, native, overlay), `kind` (ranked, fixed), `unlock_trigger`, `class_scope`, `away_safety`, `tags`, `incompatible_with` (id, optional `min_rank` / `other_min_rank`, reason), `ranks[]` (rank, heat, `params` {row, option, knobs, values}, reward, `effect_line`), `learn_text`; plus `limits` (start cap 12, max 20, mastery step 1, slots 2, no_safety rank 3).
- 12 Pacts: nine bot-side (Blood Raven's Tithe, Andariel's Shadow, Rakanishu's Bargain, Griswold's Debt, Cain's Silence, Pact of the Cursed Gold, The Hungry Dark, The Stranger's Toll, Warriv's Long Road), Pact of Rotting Bone (native, `/players`, class scope without the summoners), Pact of Nightmares (native, fixed, heat 8), Bishibosh's Wager (overlay). Left out of the 12: Pact of the Wandering Deep (overlay, parked with `pactoverlay`) and Pact of Hell (not a user decision). Ranks 1, 3 and 5 are defined (design note: 2 and 4 interpolate, U); only defined ranks can be picked. Heat per rank is the design note's heat times the rank.
- Loader (`pact.Parse`, `pact.Shipped(Refs)`): unknown field, family, kind, trigger, away class, class, policy row or option, knob (or a value outside its range), duplicate id, rank without heat, bad rank order, incompatibility naming a missing Pact: each error names the Pact and the cause (V `TestLoaderErrorsNameTheCause`). `Refs` are functions so the package imports neither the policy table nor the knob tables; the tests pass the real ones.
- Rules (`Catalogue.Check(set, class, slots, masteries)`): unknown Pact or rank, a Pact twice, class scope, slots, heat sum over the cap, a declared incompatibility (either side declares it), two `no_safety` Pacts both at rank 3 or more. Nothing below the cap is refused for being poor (V `TestHeatCapAndSlots`, `TestIncompatibility`, `TestClassScopeAndUnknowns`, Paladin and Sorceress).
- Not here: unlock events, mastery counting, the tally multiplier, the offer UI (TASK-168), away consent (TASK-169).
