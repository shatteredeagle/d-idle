## What this gives the two consumers

**Decomp / oracle work.**
- A free second and third opinion on what a function is: with three sources and our own V findings, 33 of our addresses now have a name that agrees with an oracle-backed finding (name map V table). For the rest, redivivus and recompilation names agreed in meaning on the roughly 100 most-cited rows I read, except the D rows (read by eye while building the table, not exhaustive; S).
- D2MOO/redivivus module prefixes say which old DLL a function came from, which tells where to read the D2MOO source for intent.
- Possible fix to our memory doc (not edited here, outside my files): docs/d2-1.14d-memory.md lists `dwItemFlags` twice, at 0x0C and 0x18. D2MOO (D2ItemDataStrc: 0x0C owner GUID, 0x14 command flags, 0x18 item flags) and the D2BS source our doc cites both put item flags at 0x18, so the 0x0C entry looks wrong (S; verify live before editing).
- New leads for stuck decomp questions: a named function in redivivus or recompilation is cheaper to confirm than to find; start from the S table.

**Item-verbs design.**
- The packet and validation reference (docs/research/item-packets-2026-11-12.md) gives, for every action, packet id, layout, handler address, and the gate order.
- It also surfaces one real risk for the design: the order of the two ids in packet 0x27 (identify) differs between two outside sources and our fid-identify note (item-packets doc, conflict 1). Resolve offline before any raw 0x27 is built.
