## Summary

- **Holy Bolt (V read + V run).** The missile `holybolt` (id 55, Vel 20, Range 50, CollideFriend, LastCollide, Holy) has server hit function 7 (FUN_005a9fb0) with sHitPar1 1 (heals allies) and sHitPar2 1 (undead only). A hit unit that is the caster's own pet / merc (FUN_005542c0) or an ally in the caster's party (FUN_00554d20) is **healed** by a roll of calc1..calc2 (ln12 / ln34, Prayer synergy) and the bolt ends; every other unit is **damaged only if it is undead** (return 3), and **passed through with no damage** (return 4) if it is not. Players are always passed. The undead test (FUN_0063e990: MonStats +0xd & masks 3 or 4) and the demon test (FUN_0063e940: mask 5) agree with d2d's `undead` / `demon` flags on **all 734 monster ids** (V run, 0 differ; 123 undead, 185 demon). d2d implements the filter and not the heal (its source says so).
- **Blessed Hammer (V read + V run).** Do 73 (FUN_005d0040) pays at the action frame (no start function), makes one `blessedhammer` missile (92) from the caster, sets path type 14 (FUN_00648cf0, FUN_00649970) and scales the missile's magic min / max (stats 0x34 / 0x35) by **half of the caster's Concentration damage %** (FUN_006461d0: state 42 concentration, stat 25, x Param1 4 / 8, toward zero: Concentration 1 +30 %, 10 +97 %, 20 +172 %: V run). A hit on an undead or a demon adds **+50 % of the rolled magic damage** (pSrvDmgFunc 5, FUN_005ad2f0, dParam1 / dParam2 = 50). The flight is a **spiral out of the caster's own subtile**, not a bolt at the target: V run radius 1.6 subtiles at frame 10, 4.5 at 25, 6.5 at 50, 10.4 at 75, 10.8 at 100, 13.0 at 120 (Range 120 frames), 2.5 to 2.9 turns (about 0.57 a second on average), the same at every level and every aim (the aim only sets the phase). A stationary Quill Rat standing in its orbit was hit at distances 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and **not** at 3, 7, 11 to 14 (V run, once each).
- **Damage numbers (V run, 0 differ of 8,640 cases).** Element min / max with synergies for Holy Bolt (mag, EMin 8..16, synergy (Blessed Hammer + Fist of the Heavens) x 50 %), Blessed Hammer (mag, 12..16, synergy (**Vigor + Blessed Aim**) x 14 %: the table, equal to the plan's Vigor / Blessed Aim / Concentration endgame points), Holy Fire, Holy Freeze, Holy Shock, Fist of the Heavens: game.exe (FUN_00644d50 / e40 / f20) equals d2d's `elem_damage` / `elem_length`.
- **Auras (V read + V run, 0 differ of 21,760 cases).** Do 65 (FUN_005cf010, friendly) and 66 / 81 (FUN_005cf3a0 / FUN_005d0920, Holy Fire / Shock / Freeze, Sanctuary, Conviction) read six aurastat ids and calcs, the five passive stats, the range (ln12: **Might / Prayer / Defiance / Concentration 16 + 2 per level, Vigor 16, 19, 22 .. 73 at 20, Holy Fire / Freeze / Shock 6 + 1 per level**) and the period; game.exe's calc machine equals d2d's `eval_calc` on every aura row and slot. Every Paladin aura row in the run has perdelay 50: the pulse is a scheduled event every **50 frames (2 s)**, aligned to game frames 1 mod 50 (FUN_0056cda0, FUN_0056cd50). The self state lasts to the next pulse boundary plus 2 frames, so the effect is continuous.
- **Deviations d2d vs game.exe (all absent rules, each V read in game.exe and S read in d2d, none with a differential today):** D1 Holy Bolt's heal; D2 Concentration's 50 % share of Blessed Hammer's damage; D3 Blessed Hammer's +50 % against undead and demons; D4 the hammer's path; D5 the aura's per-pulse mana (and Might's 1-point charge on the command); D6 which units an aura reaches (merc, party) is not built. **No PR candidate:** the three existing differentials (flags, element damage, aura calcs) show 0 mismatches before any change, and each deviation is a missing rule with no d2d-side harness to take mismatches before > 0 (details in section 7).
- **Bot (described, not changed):** the profile's Hammer band is sound in shape (Concentration is the aura that raises Blessed Hammer; Holy Bolt is only a prerequisite and never cast), but the resting Prayer costs mana every pulse on game.exe and holds mana regeneration off while it is paid, d2d charges nothing (B1 to B4).

**Q1. Does Holy Bolt hit non-undead monsters?** No: it passes through them with no damage and keeps flying (return 4). **V read** (FUN_005a9fb0 0x5aa112..0x5aa16c); the filter's input, the undead / demon flag, **V run** over every monster id: `tools/emu/pal_flags.py game` calls FUN_0063e990 and FUN_0063e940 on a fake monster unit for ids 0..733 and prints `id undead demon`; `pal_test --undead` prints d2d's `type_info.undead` / `demon` per game.exe unit id (`mon_bin`); the two files are byte equal (md5 0a050ae1...), 123 undead and 185 demon ids. Act 1 to 3 monsters up to level 14: undead only: skeletons, zombies, wraiths, mummies, skeleton archers and mages, Griswold; demon only: fallen, fallen shamans, corrupt rogues, goatmen, bigheads, fetishes, Blood Raven, Andariel, the Smith, the rogue archers / lancers; no row is both (D, MonStats.txt).

**Q2. Undead conversion versus heal?** There is no conversion. A friendly unit (caster's pet or merc, party ally) is healed, anything hostile is damaged (undead) or passed (the rest); an undead that is the caster's own (a Necromancer's skeleton in the party) takes the heal branch because the friendly test comes first. **V read**. The heal amount (calc1..calc2 x 256 rolled, Prayer synergy 15 % a level) has no differential and no d2d counterpart: **U** for the run; see D1.

**Q3. Mana?** Paid in the do, 2.0 to 3.2 mana per cast over levels 1..20 (above). d2d pays at the command (SKMANA D1); the bot's `ManaCost` is the same formula (SKMISS B2, B3 apply unchanged).

**Q4. Damage?** Magic damage 8..16 at level 1, 84..94 at level 10, 196..220 at level 20 with no synergy; with Blessed Hammer and Fist of the Heavens at 20 each (+2000 %) 168..336 at level 1 and 4,116..4,620 at 20 (V run, `pal_dmg.py`, clvl 30). Only undead (and, by sHitPar2 = 2 rows, demons) take it. Resistance: magic, no pierce (FUN_0057bf80, S: SKMISS).

- **Pulse (V read).** `FUN_0056cda0` schedules a skill with the aura (+4 bit 5) or periodic (bit 6) flag: `p = max(perdelay calc, 5)`, next event at `ceil(frame / p) * p + 1` as an event of type 8 with the skill and level (FUN_005417d0). Paladin perdelay is 50 for every aura row run. The do (FUN_005cf010) sets the aurastate on the caster with length `next pulse - frame + 2` (FUN_0056cd50) and the six aurastats evaluated by FUN_00646ca0; with an auratargetstate it runs `FUN_0056b7e0` (area apply by aurafilter 73731 = 0x12003 for the friendly auras, 42883 = 0xa783 for Holy Fire) over the units within aurarangecalc with the same callback (FUN_005cedc0) that puts each aurastat on the state; the callback also records the aura's skill id and level in stats 0x15e / 0x15f.
- **Mana (V read).** FUN_00644b10 = `(lvlmana x (lvl - 1) + mana) << manashift`. When the cost is > 0, the caster is a player and at least one stat was applied (the callback counter), the do puts state 0x55 (85, `nomanaregen`, D) on the caster and pays the cost with FUN_0056c110 (stat 8 -= cost when mana >= cost; its answer is ignored, so an aura that cannot pay keeps working). With a cost of 0 (every aura of this section except Prayer) it clears state 85. State 85 holds the mana regeneration off (the name, D; the regeneration code FUN_005806f0 was not re-read for it: U).
- **Holy Fire / Freeze / Shock (V read, S).** Do 66 / 81 put the skill's passive stats on the caster's aurastate and a hit of the skill's element on every enemy in range each pulse (FUN_0056e0c0, flags 0xd); Freeze adds the slow; the element roll and resistance are SKMISS's (S). The enemy damage was not run.

## 4. d2d's version (S, upstream main 72132b99)

- Holy Bolt: `Fight::skill_missile_hits` (components/game/fight/missiles.cpp:551..557): sHitPar2 1 / 2 filter by `type_info.undead` / `demon`; "ponytail: it doesn't heal the player's side". Its roll and resist are the `missile_blow` chain of SKMISS (D1 there: the roll's top 256th).
- Blessed Hammer: `Fight::cast_missile` / `fire` (:337): one missile, `target = caster`, `turn = atan2(dy, dx)`; `missile_tick` (:509..517) moves it each tick: radius = seconds x `missile_speed(info, lvl)` x 0.2 (path velocity 3,456 x 5 / 4,096 = 4.22 cells/s, so 4.2 subtiles a second), angle = start + seconds x 2 pi / 1.6. Source comment: "the client path's shape isn't traced". Hits go through `skill_missile_hits` -> `strike` with `missile.struck`: each monster is struck **once per missile**. No Concentration influence (no `concentrat` in components/rules), no pSrvDmgFunc 5 (no `dmg_func` field in `MissileInfo`).
- Auras: `Fight::aura_pulse` (fight/missiles.cpp, after `burst`) every `max(perdelay, 25)` frames from selection: Prayer's life heal and the do 66 / 81 enemy hit; `update_fighters` (strike.cpp:125..138) holds the selected aura as a permanent self state and sums its aurastats (`eval_calc`, equal to game.exe above); no mana, no merc, no state 85, no aurafilter (S: upstream "Not yet").
