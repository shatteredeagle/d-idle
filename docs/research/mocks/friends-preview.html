What is d.idle?

An idle Diablo II. Your hero plays on its own while you do something else.

  • It asks you questions when it is unsure, and learns from your answers.
  • You can send it somewhere on a map.
  • You can watch it play, or ignore it for hours.
  • Offline single-player only. You bring your own Diablo II game files.

This is an early preview with fake data. Click through the five minutes below, then tell us what you think on the last page. About 5 to 8 minutes in total.

Help


What does the hero do while I am away?
It plays on its own and keeps a journal. Questions fade away on their own and the hero does the safe thing.


Why did it ask me that?
Every question says why you are asked and what happens if you do nothing.


How do I stop it?
Pause is always one press away, at the top of the watch screen.
