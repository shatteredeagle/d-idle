## Summary

- **No fallback.** d2d builds all three (S, upstream `components/game/fight.cpp`: `start_swing` / `skill_swing` / `strike`; its `skill_built` is true for them): Zeal as a counted chain with the round-robin target pick, Sacrifice with its life price, Smite with the shield, the stun and the knockback. The "a plain attack for now" message is for other skills (S). The recon's "d2d routes unknown skills to a plain attack" does not hold for these three.
- **What game.exe does (V read, five functions):** Sacrifice start 29 = FUN_005ce790 (one hit, ED = calc1) plus do 64 = FUN_005ce8e0 (the price); Smite do 150 = FUN_005ce9f0 (no start; the shield's damage, sure hit, stun calc2, knockback by ResultFlags 8); Zeal start 37 = FUN_005daf40 (stores the hit count calc1 in the unit's skill data) plus do 13 = FUN_005dbc60 (one hit per action frame, ED = calc2, next target by id).
- **V run (this card, `tools/emu/pal2*`, seeds 7 and 1, 20,360 + 20,000 + 20,500 cases each):** the calc slots (calc1..4) and the to-hit bonus of the three skills equal d2d's `eval_calc` / `skill_tohit` on **all 40,720 cases** (0 differ). Smite's damage differs in small places (D1 to D5 below); D1 to D3 are missing rules on d2d with little reach in 1.14d gear, D4 and D5 are rounding. **No PR candidate by the rule** (the harness is new, not the existing one); D1 has a before / after measurement ready for an independent check (section 5).
- **Not proven (U):** Sacrifice's price when the target dies (which life stat the do reads), Zeal's reach radius, Smite without a shield, Holy Shield's damage on Smite, Zeal's damage through FUN_0057b7d0 (read, not run).
- **Bot (described, not changed):** the ladder takes Smite over the plain attack below clvl 12 and Sacrifice never (B2, B3); no rung checks the shield Smite needs (B1); the Zeal hold is a client repeat, the chain itself is server-side (B4).

**Q1. Which dispatch?** Skill rows route by srvstfunc (start table 0x732140) and srvdofunc (do table 0x7322b0, S upstream doc `re/` skills notes, "Zeal, Sacrifice, Smite"); the rows above give each skill's indices (D). The five functions are V read here and agree with the upstream notes line for line (S) except where D7 / D8 say otherwise.

**Q2. Hit count, AR and damage bonus.** Zeal: 2, 3, 4 hits at levels 1, 2, 3 and 5 from level 4 (calc1, V run on all levels 1..40: 0 differ); AR bonus +10 % +10 per level (level 20: 200 %); ED 0 below level 5 then 6 per level above 4, plus 12 per Sacrifice level (240 at Sacrifice 20: V run, 0 differ). Sacrifice: ED 180 + 15 per level + synergies (level 1: 180; level 20 with Redemption and Fanaticism at 20: 865), AR bonus 20 % + 7 per level, one hit. Smite: ED 15 + 15 per level on the **shield's** damage (Buckler 1 to 3 ... Zakarum Shield 46 to 46: D, armor table), no AR bonus and no miss.

**Q3. Mana and life.** Zeal and Smite: 2 mana flat (D). Zeal has a start function, so the mana is paid once per click, not per hit (S: SKMANA, docs/research/sk-mana-trace.md); Smite has no start function, so it pays in the do (S: same). Sacrifice: no mana; the price is calc2 = 8 % at every level (V run) of the physical dealt (record +8, in 256ths, post-hit) capped at the target's life: a kill of a 15 life monster by a 40 point blow costs at most 1.2 life under the reading "life before" (U, D8).

**Q4. Knockback and stun.** Only Smite has ResultFlags 8: knockback on a hit (FUN_0057cee0, S upstream notes) and a stun of calc2 ticks (record +0x44); Zeal and Sacrifice have none (D, rows). d2d builds both (S `fight.cpp:806`, `swing.stun_ticks`, `blow.stun_ticks = min(.., 250)`).

**Q5. What a held button repeats.** None of the five functions reads a button state (V read). One Zeal use stores N hits in the unit's skill data and the do runs them on the following action frames; a held left button can only repeat the use (client side, I), paying the mana again each time (S). Smite and Sacrifice are one hit per use. Next-target rule (V read of the picker FUN_0056bcd0): among the enemies the search returns, the one with the **next higher unit id** than the last target, else the lowest id (a lone enemy is hit again); d2d's `other_target` is the same rule over the monster index (S + V read, `fight.cpp:2399`).

**Q6. Which skills fall back in d2d?** None of the three (S): `skill_built` is true; `start_swing` pays the mana and sets the Zeal counter from calc1 (`kicks_left = calc1 - 1`), `skill_swing` builds Smite / Zeal / Sacrifice (`fight.cpp:794-815`), `strike` pays Sacrifice's price (`fight.cpp:1030-1037`).

## 4. Bot findings (described, not changed)

- **B1.** No rung checks the shield Smite needs: `internal/combat/classdata/profiles.json` (bands at lines 98-140: `[Blessed Hammer, Zeal, Smite, Attack]`, `[Zeal, Smite, Attack]`, `[Smite, Attack]`), `internal/combat/rules.go:237` (`data.Physical: Zeal, Smite, Sacrifice`) and `internal/combat/bindleft.go:15` list it as a plain left skill; a grep for "shield" in `internal/combat/*.go` finds only the Barbarian pair check (`gearcheck.go:70`). On game.exe a missing shield makes FUN_005ce9f0 return 0 (V read), so a cast without one is a no-op (I for what the client shows).
- **B2.** Below clvl 12 the bot's last band is `[Smite, Attack]` (profiles.json:131-137) and Smite costs 2 mana of a 15-point pool each. Smite's damage is the shield's, not the weapon's: Buckler 1 to 3, Small Shield 2 to 3, Large Shield 2 to 4, Kite Shield 2 to 5 (D), plus 15 % at level 1 (V run calc1), against the weapon's own damage on the plain attack. So Smite is the weaker damage source at low levels; its value is the stun (15 + 5 per level ticks) and the knockback, and that it never misses (I for the verdict, D and V run for the numbers).
- **B3.** Sacrifice never appears on a ladder (`build-paladin-normal30-v2.json`: one point as the Zeal prerequisite; recon section 3 "never cast before Zeal"). On game.exe level 1 gives ED 180 % and +20 % AR for a price of 8 % of the damage dealt (V run calcs, V read price); the life economics are U (D8, drill R1). Not a recommendation, a number the plan can weigh.
- **B4.** `internal/input/controller.go:375` (`HoldClick`) and `internal/combat/fighter.go:717-720` (hold only for skills flagged `hold`: Zeal) assume the hold produces the chain. game.exe stores the chain in the unit's skill data at the start (V read), so one click already yields N hits; a hold re-clicks after each swing (I). `holdMs` 600 is then the repeat interval, not the chain length (U; the 19 kills in 55 s live observation stands as measured).
- **B5.** `classdata/skills.json` ranges 7 for the three are Kolbot's (recon section 3); game.exe's reach is the skill's h2h range and d2d's is 1.6 cells (D9, U).

## 7. Dead ends

- **Smite rolls to hit (first reading, wrong).** FUN_0057ec10 is called with bonus 0 and the roll (FUN_0057d9b0) is real, so the first harness run showed d2d "never missing" against 8,631 of 20,000 game.exe misses and a drafted fix "Smite rolls". The upstream note and a second read of 0x5cebad..0x5cebb0 (`or ax, 1` right after the call) show the hit bit is forced; the candidate F1 was dropped before any report. Lesson kept: read to the join of the result, not to the call.
- Making a differential for **Zeal's damage** through FUN_0057b7d0 (the build step inside FUN_0057dbf0): not done; I read, S upstream notes agree, FUN_0057b420 on the record's ED is the same call SKMELEE2 ran (0 whole-point differences on weapons), so Zeal's number rides on that S + I, not a run of its own.
- Running FUN_005ce9f0 / FUN_005dbc60 whole: needs the party, item, target and record machinery (FUN_0057dbf0 -> FUN_0057ca80); not attempted, the calc and damage cores were run instead.
