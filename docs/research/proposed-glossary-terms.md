# Proposed glossary terms (for the user's grill session; GLOSSARY.md is a guard file and was not edited)

> status: proposals from REVIEWDOC, 2026-10-30. Each term is used in merged code or docs today but is missing from GLOSSARY.md or used with two meanings. Format follows the existing entries: term, one-sentence meaning, where it lives, what it is not.

- **slot** (d2d lab slot): one of four lockstep d2d run positions on the worker host, `tools/d2dslot.sh run -- CMD`, each with its own `/tmp/d2d-lab-slot-N.lock` (slot 0 is the legacy `/tmp/d2d-lab.lock`), socket, ports (bot 8475+N, view 8891+N) and XDG dirs. Not a Game.exe instance (`i0..i5`, docs/multi.md) and not a fleet slot (`internal/fleet`, a bot host position in the Wine container). Spec: lab-rules; card: compute.
- **tap** (d2dview tap): the one process that holds d2d's single control connection and serves two private sockets, an unfiltered one for the bot and a read-only one for the viewer (`cmd/d2dview`, `Mux`). The replay **tap** (`internal/d2dreplay/tap.go`, on agent/replay) is a different thing: a recording proxy that journals verbs and checkpoints. Suggest "view tap" and "record tap" or one shared package.
- **replay**: today two things. (1) `internal/replay` + `cmd/d2rec`: recorded Game.exe memory snapshots as fixtures and canaries (docs/replay.md). (2) `internal/d2dreplay` + `cmd/d2dreplay` (agent/replay): a gzip JSON-lines file of d2d verbs with tick numbers and state-hash checkpoints that a lockstep d2d can play back deterministically, and render to video. Suggest "snapshot replay" vs "session replay", or rename (2) to "journal".
- **checkpoint**: a line in a session replay or the d2dctl journal (`D2DCTL_JOURNAL`, detloop) carrying the tick and a hash of the game state (`dump` subset), compared on playback; a mismatch is a divergence and stops the run. Not a save point and not the 20-minute "save point" of the Game.exe soak.
- **killtime** (`killtime` log line): per-kill split of the in-game time since the previous kill into walk / cast / wait buckets by player mode plus displacement, with `gap_since_last_kill` on the runner clock (agent/killgap, docs/killgap.md); summarized by `d2dsoak extract`. Not "time to kill" of one monster.
- **gate v2**: the soak verdict rule that replaces the v1 mean-and-CI comparison for deaths with exact Poisson bounds (budget of 1 death per 6 h block, one-sided p against the baseline rate, lower-bound cap) and adds life proxies (`dmgTaken`, `hpMinPct` from the minute line). `d2dsoak compare -gate v2`. Spec: soak-gates.
- **minute line / proxy fields**: the structured per-minute log line of the bot (`minute` key=value), the only input of `d2dsoak extract`; `dmgTaken` (cumulative life points lost) and `hpMinPct` (lowest life percent in a fight that minute) are its proxy fields (minutefields).
- **TESTING label** (board): the state a card shows while its agent runs a live or soak verification rather than writing code; appears in `tools/agentstatus` (`--state`) and the hub. Not an openspec status (those are proposed / in-progress / done / archived / deferred).
- **holding card**: an openspec change with `status: deferred` kept in `openspec/changes/` so its owns and gates stay visible (gameexe-retire-soak, orig-decision-probes); it is not launched and not archived. Suggest the word "deferred card" to match the status field.
- **floating cap** (the worker host): no fixed Game.exe count; new live work is allowed when `tools/capacity.sh --add <cores>` is not NO (predicted idle+nice >= 25 %, iowait < 40 % over 5 min); a **big run** (more than one new instance, > 2 cores for > 30 min, any multi-hour soak) is announced and starts after 5 minutes of silence. docs/process/worker-capacity.md.
- **lockstep**: the fork's `--lockstep` mode where the world advances only on `step N` (N ticks of 40 ms) and `clock` reports the virtual time; the **Pacer** (`internal/d2dinput`) is the bot's clock that turns `Sleep(d)` into steps; **LateClock** (`internal/d2denv`) is the clock that becomes a Pacer once lockstep is detected and a wall clock otherwise. **Watch** (detloop) runs the runner's guard on the stepping goroutine.
- **V-d2d**: a verification tag meaning measured on the reimplementation (private fork), not on Game.exe; distinct from V (verified on the original or in code) and U. Used throughout docs/d2d-*.md; GLOSSARY lists V/S/U only.
- **frozen** (Game.exe path): per ADR 0009 no new behaviour is written for the original-game backend; it is kept building, used for the acceptance baseline and the retirement soak, and left at its last live-verified state. Not "deleted" and not "unsupported for development".
- **retirement soak**: the 24 h soak of the d2d container (card d2d-container-soak) after which Game.exe stops being the reference (ADR 0009).
- **public page / scene**: `tools/publish` output served on the internet (`index.html`, `status.md`, `scene.html`, `scene.json`); **scene** is the stream-overlay variant read by the Kick kiosk; everything in them passes the fail-closed sanitizer (`tools/publish/sanitize.py`).
- **needs-you item**: an entry in `tools/needs-you.sh` shown on the board's "Waiting on you" panel; the one place a user decision is queued (docs/needs-you.md).
- **upstream PR candidate**: a draft fix for psi29a/d2decomped kept in `docs/research/upstream-pr-candidates*.md` and a branch under `~/d2d-lab/pr/` on the worker host; nothing is pushed or posted without the user's go (AGENTS.md).
