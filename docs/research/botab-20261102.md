# BOTAB: bot-level d2d against game.exe from the records we already have (card botab, 2026-11-02)

> status: current. Offline, docs and two read-only scripts (`tools/baseline/botab_extract.py`, `tools/baseline/botab_run.py`, built on `tools/baseline/ab_compare.py`). No game, no live run, no Go change. Companion: docs/research/botab-trace.md (Q, D, B lists, limits).
> Tags: **V read** = read in a committed record or in `git diff` (named); **D** = computed by the botab scripts from committed minute lines (rerun it: command in section 7); **S** = a number copied from another doc (named), not re-derived; **I** = my inference; **U** = unverified. "d2d" = the lab fork in lockstep, "game.exe" = Game.exe 1.14d under Wine, offline, on hidden lanes of the worker host.

## 0. Bottom line

1. **No metric is EQUIVALENT within 15 percent, and none is shown DIFFERENT on XP/h.** Every XP/h row is INCONCLUSIVE (D): the intervals of the d2d/game.exe ratio are wide (for example 0.72 to 1.99 at clvl 12-13, section 4.1) because the game.exe arm has at most 3 usable lanes at the current bot code and its own spread (CV 25 percent there, 13 percent in the older runs).
2. **Kills per minute of d2d is lower than game.exe in every comparison** (ratio 0.73 to 0.78 of the game.exe mean, intervals mostly touching 1 from below; the one same-code lane pair gives 6.6 against 7.2 to 8.5 kills per minute, D). Part of this may be the way the bot counts kills (a dead monster seen in a snapshot), not the game (B1 of the trace, U).
3. **The only apples-to-apples pair in the records**: d2d gate run 1102 (bot 050a509) and the game.exe late set (bot 74abe06f66bf) run **the same bot code**: `git diff` of `internal/`, `cmd/` and `go.mod` between them is empty (V read). The other four d2d runs and the three older game.exe runs run different bots. That pair is n = 1 d2d run against 4 game.exe lanes (3 of them marked weak by the record), so it carries no interval.
4. **Not comparable as recorded**: character history (d2d plays one campaign from clvl 1; the game.exe late lanes restore a clvl 12 kit snapshot and play 2 hours), clock (d2d lockstep runs 6 to 12 virtual minutes per wall minute; game.exe k = 1), seed (d2d always seed 11, game.exe uncontrolled), and the sample (all five d2d runs are ONE seed and their early game is identical between builds, so they are not five independent samples).
5. **To answer "is d2d within 15 percent of game.exe on XP/h" the records cannot do it.** Minimum new runs, section 5: about 15 to 17 clean lanes per arm at clvl 12-13 if the per-arm CV is 10 to 15 percent (up to 37 if it stays at the 17 and 25 percent seen now), or 5 d2d lanes plus 2 more game.exe lanes for the fresh clvl 1-6 cell if the d2d CV there is at most 8 percent (it is 23 percent across builds today, so a pilot comes first).

## 2. Definitions (same for both arms)

hours = minute lines / 60 (virtual game hours; the d2dsoak convention of baselines.json); XP = net character XP over the lines (later minus earlier line), an interval is attributed to the clvl of its earlier line; kills, deaths, potions = sum of positive increments of the running counters (`kills` is the bot's lifetime counter, `potionsUsed` the drunk counter); a "town entry" is a line in a town area after a line outside town (so a game restart, which re-enters town, also counts: it inflates the number on lanes with many restarts); time per area = share of minute lines per area id. Bands are clvl bands of the line. Ratio r = mean(d2d) / mean(game.exe) over runs, 95 percent interval by the delta method on the log ratio with Welch degrees of freedom (both arms' error), verdict against margin 15 percent (`tools/baseline/ab_compare.py`, whose output is copied below). Runs flagged weak are kept out by ab_compare; I fed the late lanes both ways (all four, and without late3) and say which.

## 5. What the records cannot answer, and the minimum new runs

Gaps (each is a reason a verdict above is INCONCLUSIVE):

| # | gap | why the records cannot close it |
|---|---|---|
| G1 | game.exe arm at the current bot code: 1 clean lane (late1) and 3 weak ones | the late set has 3 of 4 lanes flagged by the restart counter; kit sets are weak for the same reason (S, tierb section 6) |
| G2 | d2d arm at the same code on a restored kit | no d2d lane started from the clvl 12 kit snapshot; the d2d gates start fresh and reach clvl 12 by their own path |
| G3 | independence | all d2d runs are seed 11 (several identical until the builds diverge); game.exe maps are uncontrolled, so the pair correlation is unknown |
| G4 | clock regime | no k = 1 d2d run; the lockstep d2d runs 6 to 12 times faster than real time, the bot's timers were designed for, the effect is not measured |
| G5 | deaths | 0 to 1 events per arm in the band; Poisson intervals span 0.001 to 13 |
| G6 | kills counter | the bot counts a kill when a monster seen alive is later seen dead in a snapshot (B1); whether both backends show the corpses for the same length of time was not checked |
| G7 | Nightmare, Hell, acts 2 to 5 | no game.exe lane; the d2d lanes are act 1 Normal |

| scenario (CV d2d, CV game.exe) | true gap 0 | gap 5 % | gap 10 % |
|---|---|---|---|
| fresh clvl 1-6, game 4 % (kitb), d2d 5 % | 4 | 5 | 13 |
| same, d2d 8 % | 6 | 7 | 22 |
| same, d2d 12 % | 9 | 12 | 42 |
| same, d2d 23 % (spread across builds today) | 23 | 36 | 137 |

| clvl 12-13, 15 % and 13 % (older game.exe runs) | 17 | 27 | 100 |

**Minimum new runs** (all with ONE bot commit on both arms, `d2cheat runstart` markers, 0 writes after start, hidden lanes only, started by the coordinator; nothing here was run):

1. **Pilot, cheapest first, fresh clvl 1-6 cell**: bot pin 15506e05 (kitb's); d2d arm 5 lanes from fresh characters, one pinned seed per lane (pinning needs AB-SEEDPIN of tierb section 4; without it use 5 different seeds), k as the lockstep, each run until clvl 7 (about 60 to 110 game minutes); game.exe arm: the existing kitb three plus 2 new k = 1 lanes from the kit snapshot (`--fresh-from-set`). That is 5 d2d + 2 game.exe runs, about 2 hours of three-lane game time. It yields the d2d CV at the pin. If the d2d CV is at most 8 percent, 6 to 7 per arm settle the cell (add 1 to 2 runs per arm), if it is 12 percent, 9 to 12 per arm, if 23 percent, 23 per arm.
2. **Late band, clvl 12-13 cell (the cell with the most weight, because it is where soaks spend their time)**: bot commit X, kit snapshot Fresha (clvl 12), two game hours per lane at k = 1 on both arms. At the CV of the older game.exe runs (13 percent) and d2d 15 percent: **17 lanes per arm** (so 16 new game.exe lanes beyond late1 and 17 d2d lanes started from the same snapshot). Lanes must be clean: restarts kept below the weak threshold (the restart counters of late2 to late4 show the harness still restarts; card kitstall merged fixes, not re-measured, U). Game.exe cost at k = 1: 17 lanes x 2 h in rounds of 3 = 6 rounds = 12 hours real; d2d: minutes per lane. If the pilot shows the CV above 17 and 25 percent the number is 37 per arm and the question should be asked on the fresh cell instead.
3. Before either: settle G4 with 3 d2d lanes at k = 1 (the fork without lockstep or with the unit clock) to see whether lockstep itself moves XP/h; this is the only way to attach a clock effect to a gap.

The 15 percent claim for kills per hour needs more (CV 17 percent in both arms gives 37 per arm), and deaths cannot be answered by any realistic count (tierb section 3.4).

## 6. Limits

- Every number is computed from committed minute lines by a script; it is not a game run (D). The three older game.exe runs and the kit sets enter as S numbers (baselines.json bands, tierb section 6).
- Five d2d runs of one seed are not five samples (G3); intervals above treat them as independent and are therefore too narrow on the d2d side.
- The late lanes are flagged weak by the record; I show both with and without the broken lane and do not hide the choice.
- "Stony Field", "cave" for area ids are the standard Diablo II level ids from memory (I), not read from levels.txt here.
- Verdicts use the ab_compare margin of 15 percent; the gate spec's 20 percent would turn A3 kills/h upper bound 0.92 into still INCONCLUSIVE (the lower bound 0.63 is outside 0.8).
- No ledger row is added: docs/coverage/ledger.tsv holds rule tests with case counts; a bot-level comparison proves no rule.
