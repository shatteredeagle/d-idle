## 2. What the gate measures and why that sets the priorities

Gate (S, `openspec/specs/fidelity-gate/spec.md`): d2d mean within `max(20 % of baseline, abs)` for XP/h, kills/h, deaths/h, deaths per 1000 kills (abs 0.5 deaths/h, 1.0 per 1000 kills). Baseline (S, `docs/d2d-soak.md` section 3): Sorceress clvl 11 to 14, XP/h 10.9k, kills/h about 500, deaths 0/h.

I (inference): the two death metrics are the loosest. With a baseline of 0 deaths/h the tolerance is 0.5 deaths/h and 1 per 1000 kills, so d2d only fails them if it is MUCH deadlier; an easier d2d passes them silently. The tight metrics are XP/h and kills/h (plus or minus 20 %). Both are rates per wall-clock hour of the bot, so anything that changes monsters met per minute, time to kill per monster or time spent walking and in town moves them proportionally. Hence the ranking weights spawn density, missile hit behaviour, damage per cast and XP per kill above monster damage.

Death consequences still matter for sustained runs (a death costs minutes and gold), but their rate is the loosest gate. Impact scores: 3 = moves XP/h or kills/h by itself, 2 = moves them through a second step (deaths, potions, route), 1 = not a gated rate.

Dependency inputs used (V, read in this repo): `docs/d2d-gaps.md`, `docs/d2d-fidelity.md` (checks 1 to 9 and the ranked deviations in section 10), `docs/d2d-probes.md`, `docs/sme/*`, `docs/plan/death-data.md`, `openspec/specs/fidelity-gate`, `docs/soak-analysis.md`, `docs/d2d-soak.md`, `docs/research/cast-trace-gameexe.md`, `docs/research/upstream-prs.md`, and the loop results in `~/code/d2lod-wt/d2dloop/docs/d2d-loop.md` (r1 to r17: harness and nav bugs only; no fidelity numbers yet, no completed replicated-soak verdict exists in the repo at this date, U).

## 7. Safest areas, surprises, limits

Safest (I): `regen_potions_stats` (mana, potions, stamina match exactly), `drops` and `death_penalty` (not gated rates; items per kill match live), `vendor_economy` (PR 127 in flight; gold is not gated), `mon_stats` (best-covered upstream, data from our MPQ), `cast_timing` once PR 128 merges. Upstream's AI module is the best checked part of the engine (177 verified of 320), which supports a decent score for wake and thinks, but those conclusions stop at the data: radius, active rooms and wake-on-hit were never measured on the original.

Surprises (V unless noted):
- The nearly untouched part is the one our damage depends on: MISSILES has 1 verified of 59 functions, while AI has 177 of 320.
- No listed upstream gap names the experience award, yet the Den (+60 %) and Blood Moor (+20 %) XP gaps are ours and open: this is a hole in both maps.
- Most of the 438 ponytails are not ours: 92 are UI and drawing, and the 35 deviations and 13 unverified items are all drawing, sound, net or file formats. About 340 simulation ponytails remain, concentrated in `ai.cpp`, `fight.*`, `world.cpp`, `rules.hpp`.
- The death gates are so loose (baseline 0 deaths/h) that the hardest-to-verify area (monster damage) matters less for the pass or fail verdict than spawn density.
- `d2d-fidelity.md` section 10 ranks monster damage and attack rate as number one ("combat danger is not validated"); by gate weight it is number 9 here because deaths are rare and the tolerance is absolute. If the plan changes to gate on deaths harder, raise `mon_melee`, `mon_ranged` and `mon_specials` to impact 3 in the JSON and rerun.

Limits: confidence and impact are my judgment from documents, not new measurements; the ten tasks are where measurement or tracing would change those numbers. The gap map counts what upstream recorded, not what is correct, so "verified" is a lower bound on trust. No game was run for this note.
