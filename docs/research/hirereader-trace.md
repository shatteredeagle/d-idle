## Answer in seven lines

1. The bot can already see on d2d: a merc exists (`dump merc` `present`), its class code, position, life pair, and `dead` while the unit exists; the gold before and after a hire or revive (`state gold=`); the hire window rows (`menu`: name, level, life, defence, cost); the revive line with its price (`menu`: "Resurrect <name>: <cost>"). Q1, Q2.
2. It cannot see: the merc's level, experience, name, hireling Id (so not the aura), seed, gear; and **a dead merc whose corpse has gone** (the dump says `present:false,dead:true` and the bot's reader reads that as "no merc"): B2. Q1.
3. So "hire happened, merc alive and following" is confirmable today for the Rogue (gold delta or free claim + `present` + `life` + distance to the player); "revive happened" is not, until B2 is fixed (a bot-side fix, no d2d change). Q3, Q4.
4. Hire price and revive price are exactly game.exe's: offers 108,000 of 108,000 equal (earlier card, `mercstats-trace.md`), revive price 256 of 256, and the new **purse differential: game.exe `FUN_00576d90` (carried gold, then stash) and the revive chain `FUN_006637b0` + `FUN_00576d90` against d2d's `rules::hire` and `rules::resurrect_merc`: 200,000 cases (8 runs of 25,000, seeds 1..4 each), 0 differ** (V run). Q5. No d2d deviation proven: **no PR candidate**.
5. Smallest d2d change: four keys in the `dump merc` object (`type`, `name`, `exp`, `level`) at one place in the dump source (about 45 bytes more JSON); an optional `hire` section lists the open window's offers with their hireling Id (the aura). Q6.
6. Bot findings B1..B5 below (Act 2/3/5 merc codes unmapped, dead-and-gone merc read as not owned, "no hire window reader" is wrong, revive quote unread, level estimate).
7. Not shown: the game.exe hire handler's own address (only the purse and price functions), the revive's life, mode and place (read, not run), anything about Game.exe's memory reader. Dead ends at the bottom.

## Q2 Hire on d2d, what the hire changes (V read of d2d, S for the probes)

- Opening Kashya's or Greiz's window calls `hire_list(act)` (ten offers, `components/game/world/commands.cpp:62-64`); a click on a row sends `cmd::Hire{offer}`; `commands.cpp:122-131` recomputes the offers, runs `rules::hire` (`rules.hpp:1817-1829`: pays `offer.cost`, sets `merc_dead=false`, `merc_seed`, `merc_type` (the hireling Id), `merc_name`, `merc_exp` = the offer's), marks the list entry hired, rolls the list again and `spawn_merc()` (`world.cpp:1233-1245`, nearest free cell beside the player). Hiring while owning a merc replaces it, no refund (S `docs/d2d-probes.md:20`; the old gear goes with it, the policy strips first). V read.
- The free Rogue: granted when Kashya's menu opens after Blood Raven (gold unchanged), forfeited by any earlier hire, even of a dead merc (S `docs/d2d-probes.md:17-19`, `internal/d2dtown/claim.go`). So "hire happened" has two signatures: a purse drop = hire; no purse change + merc appears + burial state 4 to 5 = claim. V read of the bot's claim logic; the game side of the claim is U (docs/merc-wire.md "Not verified").
- Ids and codes (D, hireling.txt and monstats.txt of the patched stack): Id 0..5 Rogue (class 271, code `RG`), 6..14 Desert Mercenary (338, `GU`), 15..23 Iron Wolf (359, `IW`), 24..29 Barbarian (561 in hireling.txt, MonStats rows 560 and 561 both code `0A`). **The aura is a function of the Id only**: Act 2 Normal Ids 6, 7, 8 are Prayer, Defiance, Blessed Aim with the same Gold (350), HP (120), Defence (45), Str, Dex, damage and base Level (9) (D). So the window row text (name, level, life, defence, cost) cannot tell the three apart: **the aura is not readable from `menu`** and needs the Id. This is why the Defiance policy of docs/merc-wire.md needs a d2d change (Q6), while "a merc was hired" does not.

## Q4 "Merc alive and following": what the bot can use

- Alive: `dump merc` `present && !dead && life[0] > 0` (V read; S live: the earlier probe saw a clvl 17 merc follow into town alive, `docs/d2d-probes.md:66`). Dead: `dead:true` while the unit exists (about 120 steps), then `present:false,dead:true` (S, `docs/forkcheats.md:12`, `debug merc kill` live).
- Following: the merc's think (`FUN_005e52d0`) keeps it within 16 of its owner while walking, runs after at over 24, teleports into the owner's room at over 100 (units: `merc_gap`, subtiles, per axis less the merc's size, (short + 2 long) / 2). d2d equals game.exe in 100,000 cases (`mercstats-trace.md` Q4, V run there; `pets-merc-trace.md`). So a sound confirmation is "gap to the player (same formula) at most 24 after the player stood or walked for a few seconds in the same area", read from the dump's merc x, y and the player's x, y. In town the merc follows too (walk mode 6 is in the rule). That is S/I on the threshold (judgment: the bot's own `mercNearDist` is 12 subtiles, `cmd/d2bot/mercwire.go:518`, U), V run on the rule it rests on.
- A merc that is far or just arrived after a portal is not "not following": the rule teleports only over 100 and runs over 24, so the confirmation must wait (the bot's wait is 6 s, `mercWaitMax`; U).

## Q5 Purse differential (V run)

Mode `hire`: random (carried gold, stash, price), prices near the gold and near gold plus stash; mode `revive`: random (gold, stash, merc level 0..255), the price from `FUN_006637b0` itself.
- Run on the worker host (idle plus nice 66 percent beforehand): `tools/emu/run-on-worker.sh --ttl 600 hrd_run.sh 300` (smoke, 2,400 cases, 0 differ), then `tools/emu/run-on-worker.sh --ttl 2400 hrd_run.sh 25000`: **hire seeds 1..4 and revive seeds 1..4, 25,000 cases each = 200,000 cases, 0 differ.** The case mix includes refusals (counted in the oracle output: 25,674 of 100,000 hire cases and 49,826 of 100,000 revive cases ended in a refusal, game.exe returning 0 and leaving both stats alone), payments from the carried gold alone and payments that reach into the stash (the first twelve lines of seed 1 show both).
- What it does not cover: the stat setters' own storage (hooked), the overflow rule of `FUN_0053ff00` (it zeroes a stat that would exceed its limit: it only fires on an addition, never on a payment, so it cannot occur in a hire; V read), and who calls the purse function for a hire (U).

## Bot findings (described, NOT changed)

- **B1 Only the Rogue code is mapped.** `internal/d2dsrc/tables.go:92` maps `RG` only; the dump's codes for the other mercs are `GU` (338), `IW` (359), `0A` (Barbarian: hireling class 561) (D, monstats `Code`; `GU` also S: `docs/forkcheats.md:11`, `debug merc hire 12` live). With a Desert Mercenary the reader gives class -1: `d2.FindMerc` (`internal/d2/merc.go:42`) finds no merc, so `HasMerc` in `internal/bot/routeguard.go:78` and `internal/bot/task.go:327` is false (the lower with-merc floors never apply), `WaitForMerc` never waits (`cmd/d2bot/mercwire.go`, `m.Alive` false), and `d2dmerc.FromResult` leaves `Class` at 0 so `ShouldReplace` (`internal/merc/policy.go:158`, the `cur.Class == ClassDesert` branch) treats the Desert Mercenary as a non-Desert merc and would swap it for any acceptable offer at the next difficulty's first Lut Gholein visit. Fix is bot-only: map `GU`, `IW`, `0A` to 338, 359, 561 and keep 560 out as `d2/merc.go` says.
- **B2 A dead merc whose corpse is gone is read as "no merc".** `internal/d2dsrc/player.go:381-389` returns when `present` is false before reading `dead`, `Extras.Merc` stays nil, and `internal/d2dmerc/d2dmerc.go:67-69` answers `Owned=false`. The dump says `present:false,dead:true` from about 120 steps after the death (`docs/forkcheats.md:12`; the d2d side `devctl_dump.hpp:214`). So `ReviveStep` (`cmd/d2bot/mercwire.go:565`, `!st.Owned` returns at once) never revives on d2d, the `merc.AliveHook` answers "unknown" (`internal/merc/policy.go:260`), and the death counters see no dead merc at all after the window. No test fixture holds `present:false,dead:true` (`internal/d2dsrc/testdata`, `internal/d2dmerc/d2dmerc_test.go:109,129` only flip a present merc). Bot-only fix: read `dead` in the `!present` branch and set `Owned` when `dead` is true (a save with a dead merc looks the same; the free Rogue is forfeited then too, S `docs/d2d-probes.md:19`).
- **B3 "No hire window reader" is wrong.** `internal/d2dmerc/d2dmerc.go:21` (`ErrNoOffers`), `d2dmerc.go:57`, `cmd/d2bot/mercwire.go:50-52` and `:228`, and docs/merc-wire.md (lines 14, 36, 62, 65) say the rows are only visible on screen. The rows are in `menu` (Q1; S live `docs/d2d-probes.md:16,65`; `docs/d2d-backend.md:129`; the parser and a test already exist). What is missing is the aura (Q2), not the window. A bot-side `Hirer` over `menu` (open the NPC, click "hire", parse the rows, click a row) is possible today for level, life, defence, cost; the policy's aura check needs the Q6 change.
- **B4 The revive quote is not read.** `internal/d2dmerc/act.go:64-68` only checks that a "resurrect" line exists; the line carries the price ("Resurrect <name>: <cost>", V read of d2d, Q1). `docs/merc-wire.md:61` ("no pre-payment quote read exists on d2d") is wrong. Reading it gives the exact pre-check price with no merc level and no d2d change; compare it with `merc.ReviveCost` of the level the dump would carry (Q6).
- **B5 The revive pre-check uses the player's level.** `cmd/d2bot/mercwire.go:146-153` (`levelEstimate`): the merc's level is `clvl - 5 + rand(5)` at the hire plus what its owner's kills gave it (mercstats Q3), so the estimate overstates the price (V read of the formula, S of the difference); B4 removes the need.
