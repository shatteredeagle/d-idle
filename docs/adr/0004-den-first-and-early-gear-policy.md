# 0004: Den of Evil first, free skill point, no early vendor gear

## Context and Problem Statement

Fresh characters died or looped in Act 1 on unwearable armor, gold burn and wrong areas (G1 attempts 7 to 14). The Den is optional in the game but gives a skill point and safe early XP.

## Considered Options

* Skip the Den and level in the field
* Den always first

## Decision Outcome

Den of Evil always comes first (user, 2026-10-30), then leveling in Cold Plains and Stony Field, Blood Raven at the kit gate, free Rogue. No vendor gear below the early gear cap, no armor rebuy loop, rejuvenation capped at 3 (spec early-game-policy).

## Consequences

Slightly slower first hour; far fewer early deaths in the audit.
