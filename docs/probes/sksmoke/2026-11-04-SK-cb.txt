Problem of the 2026-11-03 run: `monsters` life moves by the SUM of the bolts that hit in a cast. Method that solves it (V, ran): step ONE frame at a time after the cast and read, every frame, the `monsters` life of the target (#17) and the chargedbolt rows of `debug missiles`. A bolt that hits is removed from the list in that frame (V: bolts vanish with ttl 30-50 left, the survivors vanish at range end with no life change). So per frame: k = rows before minus rows after, d = life drop. d > 0 with k = 1 is an EXACT per-bolt sample; k >= 2 gives d / k, and over all hits mean = total drop / total bolts that hit (unbiased). A vanish with d = 0 is a miss or range end, discarded. Single-bolt frames are 64-100 percent of the hit frames (level 20 64, level 1 100), so the per-bolt range check is exact on those.
