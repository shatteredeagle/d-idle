Panels

Click to add or remove from this viewport. Each viewport keeps its own layout.

Keys

Tab to a panel. Arrows move, Shift+arrows resize, C collapses, Delete removes. Drag the title bar; drag the gold corner to resize. Snaps to 12 columns and 48 px rows; a red ghost means it would overlap and will be pushed down.
