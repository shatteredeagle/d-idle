# Necromancer smoke test (agent CLASSNEC, 2026-10-28)

Report-only run of the current bot's building blocks on a throwaway softcore Necromancer ("Necci", instance i2, Normal, 1.14d, Blood Moor). No shared package was edited. Probe code: `tools/rigs/classsmoke/necro.go` and `necro_script.go` (new files only; hooked in through `init`, so `main.go` is untouched): `ntree`, `ncheat`, `nunits`, `nstates`, `nwatch`, `nsummon [a|b|c|d|e]`. Everything else used `combatclass` (state, bind, setleft, farm), `oog`, `tools/shot.sh`. Tags: **V** observed live (how stated), **U** not verified (code reading or not run). Format follows `smoke-paladin.md`.

Cheats (ledger actor `classnec`, all on Necci, offline, with reasons): one save edit to clvl 20 (vit 60, ene 40, str 30; skills Amplify Damage 1, Teeth 6, Bone Armor 2, Skeleton Mastery 2, Raise Skeleton 3 (+1 wand), Corpse Explosion 2, Clay Golem 1, Iron Maiden 1, Raise Skeletal Mage 1, Bone Spear 1); several `heal` (mana refill during probes, restore after the death probe); two `setstat stat=6 value=512` (life 2 for the death probe). The cheat allow-list is no longer a blocker: `classsmoke` appends "Necci" in process, and `D2CHEAT_ALLOW` / `~/.config/d2lod/cheat-allow.txt` exist now.

## What the bot needs for a Necromancer to play unattended (8 steps)

1. **Minion identity (d2 + combat + bot, BLOCK 1).** `Unit.Minion` from the state-105 child list, the monster class set (`necroskeleton`, `necromage`, golems, `revive`/valkyrie via the "appeared within 3 s of our own cast" rule) and, later, an owner read once one is found (UnitAny owner words are 0). Use it in `Hostiles`, `hostileMissiles`, `bot/observe.go`, `d2bot/display.go`, boss and telemetry. Verify with a golem and two skeletons standing next to the fighter: `Hostiles` must list none of them.
2. **Necromancer build (leveler, BLOCK 3).** `build-necromancer-normal20.json` plus `defaultBuild["necromancer"]`: first point Teeth, energy before vitality early, Maxroll order from necromancer.md section 3; keep the skill-id proof (`ErrSkillMismatch`) the Paladin work added. Check with `levelerlive spend` on a clvl 6 and a clvl 13 Necci.
3. **Corpse model (d2 + combat, finding 9).** Corpse list = monster units in mode 12 near the player, minus ones the army already used; kill bookkeeping by mode so raised/exploded kills still count.
4. **Summon upkeep (combat, BLOCK 2 + finding 10).** Per profile: wanted skeleton/mage/golem counts, cap from skills.txt, walk to 4 subtiles, F5 + click on the corpse, verify the census; Clay Golem when no `claygolem` stands; recount after every area change, game restart and respawn; never in town.
5. **Curses and Bone Armor by state (d2 + combat, findings 4-6).** `Player.States`/`Unit.States` read (state 14 armor, 9 Amplify Damage ...), curse the pack's best target once and recast when the monster's state is gone, armor recast on a missing state, `ActiveAura` filtered to aura skills.
6. **Profile and mana (combat/classdata + resource, findings 7-8).** Cost-aware ladder (Teeth while Bone Spear is level 1), layout with Corpse Explosion, Clay Golem and Skeletal Mage, kinds `curse`/`corpse`, Corpse Explosion on a corpse beside two or more hostiles; skip rest-before-pack while the army fights, regen from the measured 0.5 mana/s, wand melee only as the last resort.
7. **Town and merc (town, resource, leveler prefs, findings 12, 13, 15, 16).** Hire at Greiz with a per-difficulty aura config (Normal: Prayer or Defiance), a "necromancer" prefs row (wand/staff/orb, a shield allowed), belt hp hp mp mp (already), sentinel character map, walk/Staff fallback for Teleport steps.
8. **Prove it unattended (live, finding 14).** `combatclass farm` for 10 minutes with the new loop (kills per minute above the 6.5 baseline, no minion attacked, army size over time), a real death and respawn with minions, an Act 1 clvl 1 to 20 soak through `d2bot` with the leveler on. Needs i2 and a character; the baseline numbers are in findings 7 and 10.

Order of work: 1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4 (it plays), 5 -> 6 (it plays well), 7, 8.
