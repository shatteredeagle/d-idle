   D2BOT_UPDATE_CHECK=off in .env to switch it off. Updates are installed only after a signature check against a
   key baked into the image, and only when you ask.
5. No warranty. This is a beta for a few friends. It can fail. Your hero lives in a Docker volume on your
   machine; stopping with ./didle stop saves it.
6. Sharing. This pack is private. Do not post it publicly or forward it without asking the person who sent it.
